# What happens to my answers?

*For pupils. The same words are shown in the SPAG Buddy app, with a "Read this to me" button. The wording is in `Packages/SPAGCore/Sources/SPAGCore/Services/PrivacyNotice.swift`, so change both together.*

## What SPAG Buddy knows about you

Your first name, your animal picture and your year group. It does not know your surname, where you live, your birthday or what you look like.

## What it remembers

The questions you answer, what you typed or tapped, and whether it was right. This helps Buddy choose the best questions for you.

## If you are in a class

**Who can see it.** Your teacher can see your answers so they can help you learn. Other children cannot see your answers or your stars.

**Where it goes.** Your answers are sent safely to your school's SPAG Buddy account when the iPad is online. They are never sold or used for adverts.

**Deleting it.** Your school deletes your answers after you leave the class, and always after two years. You or a grown-up at home can ask your teacher to delete them sooner.

## If you practise at home

**Where it goes.** Nowhere! Your answers stay on this iPad. Nobody else can see them.

**Deleting it.** A grown-up can delete your profile in the grown-ups' settings. Then everything is gone from this iPad.

## Worried about something?

Talk to your teacher or a grown-up you trust. You can always ask what SPAG Buddy knows about you.
