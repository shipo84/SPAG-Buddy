## Who we are

SPAG Buddy is an iPad app that helps primary pupils practise spelling, punctuation and grammar from the National Curriculum for England. It is provided by [operator name, address, company number] ("we").

When a school uses SPAG Buddy, **the school is the data controller** and we are its **data processor**. We only use pupils' information to provide SPAG Buddy to the school, under a written data processing agreement. Questions about how the school uses pupil data should go to the school's Data Protection Officer: [school DPO contact].

## What we collect

### About pupils

| Data | Why | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First name or nickname, as typed by the teacher | So the pupil can find themselves on a shared iPad, and the teacher can read reports | Teacher |
| Animal picture and 4-digit PIN (the PIN is stored only as a one-way hash) | So pupils can sign in without an email address or password | Generated by SPAG Buddy |
| Class and year group | To choose questions at the right level | Teacher |
| Answers: question, the answer typed or tapped, whether it was right, time taken, whether a hint was used, date and time | To choose the next questions and to show the teacher what the class needs | Pupil, via the app |
| A random device token (stored only as a hash) | To keep the iPad linked to the pupil without re-entering the PIN | Generated by SPAG Buddy |

We do **not** collect surnames, dates of birth, UPNs, gender, ethnicity, SEN status, email addresses, photos, voice recordings, contacts or location. Pupils cannot chat with anyone, share anything or see other pupils' results. There are no adverts, in-app purchases or third-party analytics or tracking tools in the app.

Questions are read aloud using the iPad's own built-in voice. No audio is recorded or sent anywhere.

### About teachers

Name, school email address, school name and URN, the classes you teach, and a log of important actions such as deleting pupils or resetting PINs.

## Lawful basis

For maintained schools and academies, the school's lawful basis is normally **public task** (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(e)): teaching and assessing the curriculum. Independent schools will usually rely on **legitimate interests** (Article 6(1)(f)). SPAG Buddy does not process special category data. Consent is not needed, but we recommend schools tell parents that SPAG Buddy is in use.

## How long we keep it

See [retention-policy.md](retention-policy.md). In short: answers are deleted 24 months after they were given; archived classes are deleted with all their pupils and answers 12 months after archiving; teachers can delete a pupil or a whole class at any time and the deletion is immediate.

## Your rights

Parents, carers and pupils have rights to access, correct and erase pupils' data, and to object to or restrict its use. Because the school is the controller, please make requests to the school. Teachers can:

- **Access:** download every answer for the class as a spreadsheet (CSV) and filter it to one pupil.
- **Correct:** rename a pupil.
- **Erase:** delete a pupil, or a whole class, from the teacher website. This removes their answers and unlinks their iPad.

We will help the school respond within one month.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint) or 0303 123 1113.

## Home use

Families can use SPAG Buddy at home without joining a class. In that case nothing leaves the iPad: no account is created and we receive no data. A grown-up can delete the profile in the app's grown-ups' settings.
